Soft range matching

Paper and code

Job boards treat salary and experience as yes/no filters. A posting that pays 3% under your floor disappears, and a narrow search can come back empty. A graded score would keep those postings, but a vector index ranks only by vector similarity, so systems run such scores on a short list after retrieval and never score what the list left out.

This method encodes a range and its Gaussian tolerance as 2K + 1 Fourier coefficients. The inner product of a query vector and a posting vector then equals the smoothed share of one range inside the other, eq. (1), to within ε = 10−3. One inner product per posting ranks by keywords and range fit together, and the error bound lets a flat scan prove its top 10 exact.

F(A, B; σ) = 1|A| ∫A∫B φσ(x − y) dy dx

σ2 = sA2 + sB2,   |⟨u, v⟩ − F| ≤ ε

A: the posting's pay band. B: the pay you accept. s: each side's slack. (1)
